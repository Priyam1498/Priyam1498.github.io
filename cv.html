Academic background and experience

I am a PhD researcher in quantum information science with a background in physics and scientific computing. My work focuses on variational quantum algorithms for sensing and metrology, and reinforcement learning for quantum-network control.

Research expertise

My research combines quantum-information methods, physical modeling, numerical simulation, and optimization. I work primarily on quantum sensing, multiparameter estimation, and quantum-network control, with broader interests in quantum algorithms, quantum simulation, and distributed quantum sensing.
